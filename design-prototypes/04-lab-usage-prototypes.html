CER space occupied, by lab group

Stacked area — shows both the total space in use across all groups and each group's share of it, month by month. Best for spotting overall growth alongside who's driving it.

Units occupied per lab group, over time

One line per lab group on a shared axis — the clearest way to directly compare trajectories and see who is climbing, flat, or shrinking relative to everyone else.

Space usage heatmap

Every lab × every month in one grid, shaded by units occupied. Fastest way to scan for outliers and seasonal spikes without reading a single axis.
